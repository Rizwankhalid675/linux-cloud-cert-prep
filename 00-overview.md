# Overview: your path through the four credentials

You already run production Linux: Node.js services under PM2, Nginx reverse proxies, Docker, cron jobs and SSH on live servers. This guide turns that experience into credentials employers recognize. It is ordered so each step builds on the last and gives you a visible result early.

**You take every exam and assessment yourself.** This guide gives you the plan, the labs and the practice. The badges only mean something because you earned them.

## Recommended order

| Step | Credential | Why this order | Rough time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cisco NetAcad Linux Unhatched / Essentials | Free quick win; badge on Credly within weeks | 2-3 weeks |
| 2 | Linux Foundation LFCS | Ubuntu-based, closest to your servers | 6-8 weeks |
| 3 | Red Hat RHCSA | The most respected; adds the RHEL skills | 6-8 weeks |
| 4 | AWS SysOps Administrator - Associate | Puts your Linux ops on AWS | 6-8 weeks |

LFCS and RHCSA overlap by roughly two thirds. Doing them back to back means the second one mostly covers the distro differences: apt vs dnf, netplan vs nmcli, ufw vs firewalld, AppArmor vs SELinux.

## How to use each chapter

1. Read **At a glance** and **Objectives map**. The "Your current strength" column shows where your job already covers you (Strong) and where to spend your time (Refresh, New).
2. Build the lab from **Lab setup** once, then work through the **Hands-on tasks** without looking at the solutions. Check yourself with the **Verify** commands.
3. Do the **Practice questions** in one sitting, timed. Anything you miss goes back into the week's plan.
4. Book the exam only when every box in the **Readiness checklist** is ticked.

## Ground rules that protect your certificates

- Use only official objectives, official docs and original practice material. Never use brain-dump sites: they break every provider's terms and can get a certification revoked.
- Performance-based exams (LFCS, RHCSA) test what you can actually do on a live system, so the labs matter more than the reading.
- Check prices, versions and retake rules on the official pages before booking. Each chapter lists its sources and when they were checked.
- Add each badge to LinkedIn (Licenses & certifications) and your GitHub profile README once it's issued, using the verification link Credly provides.
